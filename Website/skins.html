what a skin can do

new paint. same controls.

can change

color + material

Backgrounds, gradients, shape, corner treatments, shadows, visual materials, and light/dark variants.

can change

icons + feedback

Visual icons, control-state styling, labels, and safe haptic presentation assigned through semantic roles.

cannot change

what a tap does

Keyboard shortcuts, pointer actions, controller outputs, launch targets, and executable bindings stay local.

cannot replace

native interaction

Controls remain native and accessible. Artwork decorates them without taking over hit testing or VoiceOver.

for creators

put your skin in the directory.

Scaffold editable JSON and SVG against a canonical artboard, compile it, inspect every native state, then submit the exact package for critique and human approval. The directory checks identity, hashes, compatibility, archive limits, originality, visual quality, and appearance-only boundaries before anything ships.

thumble skin scaffold "My Skin" --identifier com.creator.my-skin -o ./my-skin
thumble skin compile ./my-skin -o ./my-skin/build/MySkin.pocketpad --clean --strict
thumble skin quality ./my-skin --strict
thumble skin preview ./my-skin --all-variants --all-states --contact-sheet -o ./my-skin/reviews/contact-sheet.png
