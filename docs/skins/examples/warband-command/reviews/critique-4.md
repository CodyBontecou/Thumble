## Pass 1 — composition

The bank-reference ledger is absent throughout all four banks, both orientations, both appearances and all four states. Former ledger areas show uninterrupted ground, with no remnant headings, miniatures, input keys, panel edges or substitute decoration. The larger quiet space in landscape separates the camera instrument from the utility tier; it does not disrupt the playable ability clusters. Portrait retains its independent vertical composition and existing canonical spacing.

The clean landscape render has no app-owned offline toolbar. It retains the native chevron system toggle, View/Menu, shoulder inputs and other functional controls. WARBAND/WARRIOR identity remains intact, as requested by revised art direction. This assessment distinguishes the removed app toolbar from the retained native control row.

Removing the ledger makes the playable icons more dominant. Their diamond layouts and adjacent input tags remain clear, without clipped corners, graphic tangencies or displaced sockets. There is no reason to reintroduce decorative content into the cleared space.

## Pass 2 — material and craft

Light remains a pale matte field with readable dark legends; dark remains a charcoal field with warm frame edges and bright ability artwork. No raster seams, patch boundaries, mismatched cleared-area colors, new blur, or stretched portrait artwork are visible. Native icon perimeters remain exposed and consistently framed.

Centered Move/Look labels retain clear separation from the rings; the previously corrected label/ring collision has not returned. All functional text and branding remains readable. The original sparse framing and socket grammar continues to look intentionally authored after the passive ledger is removed.

## Pass 3 — interaction

All four native banks retain visibly different icon assignments and bank accents. LT-only and RT-only panels mark the held trigger with HELD; both-trigger panels mark both. Base retains HOLD instructions. This supplies textual modifier context after removal of the passive bank map.

Normal, pressed, active and disabled ability states remain distinct. Pressed uses the visible contrasting pressure perimeter/inset treatment; active exposes the broader bank-colored boundary; disabled artwork is subdued while input letters remain legible. Utility controls and sticks retain their own state hierarchy. Icon centers, input tags and native geometry remain stable across the matrix. No functional control appears to have been removed as part of the decoration cleanup.

## Originality / trade-dress check

No new artwork was introduced. The authored sparse framing, typography and input-letter treatment remain distinct from console hardware or Blizzard HUD framing. Existing attributed Blizzard ability icons remain the explicit asset exception recorded in art direction. Decluttering creates no new originality concerns.
