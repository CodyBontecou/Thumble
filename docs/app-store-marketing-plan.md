# Thumble App Store gaming marketing plan

Direction updated October 19, 2026: position Thumble as an **iPhone gaming controller**, with WoW Forever fans as the first audience. Lead with the experience of holding a controller and playing a character. The opening message is **Your iPhone. Your controller.**

The current first three drafts are in [the gaming asset folder](../marketing/app-store/gaming/README.md). The earlier productivity set is superseded.

## Audience and product story

Speak to WoW Forever players interested in touch controller play, controller layouts, and setups for different characters or builds. Show recognizable gamepad controls immediately: two sticks, D-pad, face buttons, shoulders and held triggers. Explain the Mac receiver requirement in a readable supporting line.

The primary visual is Thumble's native WoW Forever controller wearing the existing Fury Forge skin, captured on a portrait iPhone. Fury Warrior, Fury — Solo and Fury — Group illustrate manually saved setups. Thumble selects its own controller profile; character, class, talents and ability assignments remain in WoW. [WoW Forever template](wow-forever.md).

Thumble Link is a separate later story: export the supported client's keyboard bindings and import them into a phone setup. Keep that workflow explicit when showing it; the addon does not provide live cooldowns, automatic ability pictures or native gamepad-layer synchronization. [Thumble Link](thumble-link.md).

## Art direction

Use vertical App Store compositions at 1206 × 2622, with genuine portrait controller captures. Start with dark charcoal, restrained crimson atmosphere, warm ivory type and muted brass accents. Place one strong benefit above a large native portrait controller capture. Keep the ability icons visible and retain the full UI aspect ratio. Preserve the real UI without relabeling its buttons in postproduction.

Keep the visual language grounded in gaming and character builds. Use original abstract backgrounds and normal text references to WoW Forever. Use the unchanged Fury Forge package and its existing Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, Execute and Cleave icon assets. Do not add invented gameplay, an official game logo, generated spell icons or live status overlays to screenshots. A new community skin would be a separate skin-authoring task with the repository's required reviews and exact-package human approval.

## Headers and promotional images

Use one unmistakable landscape controller as the header's focal point. Keep its sticks and primary controls inside Apple's template safe area. A short line such as **Your iPhone. Your controller.** can support it when the placement permits. The small search creative must still communicate gaming controller use at a glance.

Headers and search Creative Assets are optional iOS/iPadOS 27+ enhancements. Dedicated header: 3840 × 1646. Dedicated search: 3:2, up to 3840 × 2560. Universal master: 5244 × 2950 PNG. Verify final upload dimensions against Apple's active validator. [Creative specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/creative-assets-specifications), [asset guidance and templates](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/).

External promotion should show an iPhone held in landscape, a real controller layout and, where available, actual authorized gameplay on the Mac. Proposed campaign concepts:

- **Your iPhone. Your controller.** Broad gaming introduction with WoW Forever as the demonstrated setup.
- **Your character. Your setup.** Manually saved character/build controller profiles.
- **Bring your bindings with you.** A separate Thumble Link demonstration with its required export/import steps.

Adapt each concept to 1200 × 630, 1080 × 1350 and 1080 × 1920 for external campaigns. These are production choices, not Apple upload specifications. Use supplied Apple device imagery and badges according to the [marketing guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/). Match beta/public calls to action to actual availability.

## Production and acceptance

Capture the final shipping UI with the real WoW template and real configured profiles. Use original or appropriately authorized gameplay and imagery. Store captures, editable source, copy, export dimensions, source build information and hashes together.

Current drafts use the actual native iPhone UI in Practice Mode. Native gamepad signing, WoW recognition and live gameplay remain pending in the [controller acceptance documentation](virtual-controller-support.md) and [WoW setup notes](wow-forever.md). Before public submission, verify the signed receiver in the intended Forever client: both sticks, buttons, trigger combinations, targeting, menus, profile/orientation changes and disconnect releases. Capture approved live evidence before using gameplay, latency, compatibility or successful synchronization as a marketing claim.

The current Fury-profile examples are manual names on duplicated controller layouts. Fury Forge icon positions are suggested action-bar assignments; the skin does not bind spells or synchronize ability data from WoW. Do not imply automatic class/talent detection, spell synchronization or automatic talent switching. No pending community skin is approved or published by this plan.

## Growth tests

Start with the controller-led set. Test the first screenshot's headline and composition, then the header, using mobile [Product Page Optimization](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/). Evaluate Apple's conversion results and uncertainty before declaring a winner.

A WoW-focused [Custom Product Page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/) can later serve matching community/campaign links. Keep the app's general gaming-controller promise understandable to people who do not play WoW. Use paired setup success and first successful gameplay input, where privacy-respecting measurement is available, alongside download conversion.
