# Physical-device review evidence

After installing the final TestFlight candidate, record a short video with a second camera that shows both the physical iPhone and Mac:

1. Open the downloaded signed receiver and scan its fresh pairing QR code.
2. Show the standard controller, start a session, and press a few controls while a controller-input viewer or supported game responds on the Mac.
3. Pause, show that setup/local practice does not consume demo time, and resume.
4. Open Pro, show the real store price, complete a sandbox purchase, and show verified unlimited access on the paired receiver.
5. Restore Purchases with the same store account and confirm the receiver remains unlocked.
6. Briefly show custom layout delivery and dictation if practical. If cloud is included, demonstrate actual sign-in and delivery separately.

Keep passwords, Apple ID details, receipts and unrelated desktop content out of the recording. Do not use a fake store result or a mocked HID device as review evidence. Upload only after inspecting the exact video.

Apple's reviewer instructions: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/complete-review/
