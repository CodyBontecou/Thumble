# Thumble pricing and availability

The Mac receiver download, setup and local offline practice are free. The phone app is coming soon. Its demo provides 30 minutes total of connected controller use, shared across sessions, without a daily refill. Pro is planned at $19.99 USD as a one-time in-app purchase that removes the connected-use time limit. No subscription. Local store prices may vary.

No API key or paid subscription is required to read [public service configuration](https://thumble.io/v1/config). Hosted account reads require OAuth consent. The free [synthetic sandbox](https://thumble.io/v1/sandbox) requires no account, key or credit card and cannot access real devices. [Product and pricing](https://thumble.io/#pricing) · [Setup](https://thumble.io/docs).
