# Thumble pricing and availability

Every plan includes every feature. Weekly is $0.99 USD and Yearly is $29.99 USD; both start with a 7-day free trial. Lifetime is a $49.99 USD one-time purchase that never renews. Family plans for up to six people (Weekly $1.99, Yearly $49.99, Lifetime $89.99) use Apple Family Sharing and are available on the App Store only. Local store prices may vary. Earlier one-time Thumble Pro purchases carry over as Lifetime.

The Mac receiver download and setup are free. Thumble for Android is on Google Play; the iPhone app is coming soon.

No API key or paid subscription is required to read [public service configuration](https://thumble.io/v1/config). Hosted account reads require OAuth consent. The free [synthetic sandbox](https://thumble.io/v1/sandbox) requires no account, key or credit card and cannot access real devices.
