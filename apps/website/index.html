Questions

Does the game run on my phone?

No. Your game runs on your computer, and your phone is the controller. Thumble doesn’t stream video. It sends gamepad input to your computer over Wi-Fi.

Which games work?

Games on your computer that support an Xbox controller. Some need controller support turned on in their settings first; the game guides show you how.

Can I change the layout?

Yes. Start from the Xbox layout, then move, resize, remap or add controls, and save a layout for each game.

When is the iPhone app coming?

Soon. The Android app is on Google Play now. Leave your email and we’ll tell you when the iPhone app is on the App Store.

Does it work on Windows or Linux?

Yes, in preview. Free receivers for Windows 10/11 and Linux (x64) are available now, while real-game, Steam and Proton testing continues. Get the receivers.

How do I cancel a trial?

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won’t be charged.
