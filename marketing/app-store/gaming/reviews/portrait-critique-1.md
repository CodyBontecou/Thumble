# Portrait marketing critique 1

Verdict: **visual-pass for draft marketing reuse**. No blockers or unresolved major findings. This verdict does not approve the skin, grant rights to the existing ability artwork, or establish live gameplay verification.

## Evidence inspected

- Exact current `gaming-contact-sheet.png` and all three `exports/en-US/screenshots/*.png`.
- Each exported PNG measures 1206 × 2622, verified with `sips`.
- Existing immutable native skin evidence: `docs/skins/examples/fury-forge/reviews/contact-sheet-2.png`, covering portrait/landscape, light/dark, and normal/pressed/active/disabled. The current marketing task reuses that skin; it does not author a replacement.
- Acceptance direction: `portrait-art-direction.md`. Current copy intentionally emphasizes Fury Warrior rather than the earlier general movement headline.

## Pass 1: composition

All three files are genuinely portrait compositions. Shoulder/utility controls sit above the movement and camera controls, with D-pad and four ability images below. They match the portrait arrangement in native evidence rather than a rotated landscape bitmap. The phone outline and controller stay intact, with bottom breathing room and no editorial overlays across controls. The four colored ability images remain individually visible in the contact sheet and distinguish this from a generic lettered gamepad. Screenshot 3's real profile menu leaves all four icons visible.

**Minor — repeated hero framing, screenshots 1 and 2.** Visible evidence: both use the same full-height controller capture at the same size, so the second card changes copy but adds little visual information. Likely cause: preserving an intact native portrait capture across both compositions. Optional correction: give screenshot 2 an explicitly framed secondary crop of the native ability cluster, with each icon at least 40 px wide in a 300 px export preview, while keeping the full controller on screenshot 1. This is an optional campaign variation, not a requirement to complete the user's skin/orientation correction.

## Pass 2: material and craft

Charcoal, ivory, muted gold, and restrained crimson agree with the existing steel skin. Headline contrast and line breaks are strong at contact-sheet size. The phone frame is subtle; no fantasy decoration competes with the app. Native control edges and ability imagery show no visible stretching, compositing seams, or new clipping. The underlying portrait proportions agree with the prior all-state evidence. Small supporting copy is subordinate and requires opening a screenshot to read comfortably, while the headline remains legible at approximately 300 px wide.

## Pass 3: interaction and truthful presentation

Practice Mode remains visible. There is no invented gameplay scene, connected state, live spell feedback, or auto-sync indicator. Screenshot 3 shows manually named Fury Warrior, Fury — Solo, and Fury — Group profiles; no Frost Mage claim appears. “Create and switch controller profiles” does not imply automatic class detection or spell binding. Screenshot 1 states the Mac requirement. The existing state evidence distinguishes normal, pressed, active, and disabled without a layout change; the marketing images do not need to reproduce the diagnostic state grid.

## Originality and trade dress

No new copied hardware shell, manufacturer glyphs, logos, character render, or game background appears in this marketing composition. The existing Fury Forge control arrangement and its four existing Blizzard ability images are reused. These game images are third-party artwork, not original Thumble art; retain source provenance and attribution in the asset documentation. This is a visual assessment of reuse, not legal clearance. No official endorsement is claimed. Existing human approval remains pending.
