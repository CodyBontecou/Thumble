Use the existing Fury Forge skin unchanged to make the real Thumble controller immediately recognizable to WoW players through its steel framing, restrained crimson accents, and four authentic ability images. The iPhone controller is the hero; the message remains gaming, movement, and manually saved character setups.

## Capture direction

Capture the real iPhone app in **portrait orientation**, with Fury Forge applied to the canonical WoW controller profile in an isolated capture environment. Do not rotate the old landscape bitmap into a portrait export. Do not replace ability images in postproduction. The native portrait skin already recomposes shoulders at the top and movement/camera plus D-pad/ability groups below.

Use dark appearance for the main controller and a real saved-profile menu for the setup screenshot. Keep screenshots truthful about connection or Practice Mode state; do not erase status UI or claim live gameplay evidence. Keep the four ability icons clear of menus and overlays in at least screenshots 1 and 2. All app UI must come from the native capture, including visible control state and icons.

## Three compositions

1. **Your iPhone. Your controller.** One large intact portrait controller capture below a short headline. Give the lower ability group enough scale to remain recognizable in the contact sheet. Keep the whole controller silhouette, top utility controls, and bottom safe area visible.
2. **Move. Look. Take control.** Use the same genuine portrait controller UI with an intentional closer scale if needed. Supporting editorial labels may identify movement, camera, and ability buttons outside the capture; never cover, redraw, or reposition native controls. Any secondary detail crop must be explicitly recognizable as a crop of the same capture.
3. **Your character. Your setup.** Show the genuine profile selector over the portrait Fury Forge controller. Prefer truthful Fury Warrior naming; avoid implying Frost Mage artwork is supplied when the visible unchanged skin is Fury Warrior. Saved-profile names are manual choices, with no automatic class detection or spell binding claim.

## Export and acceptance

- Produce three opaque sRGB PNGs at **1206 × 2622**; each file itself must be portrait.
- Use restrained charcoal backgrounds, ivory headlines, and crimson accents that agree with Fury Forge. Avoid decorative fantasy framing competing with the real UI.
- Preserve native image aspect ratio. A full portrait capture can occupy roughly 850–930 px width and 1850–2020 px height beneath the headline; tune the actual placement to avoid a crowded bottom edge.
- At a 300 px wide export preview, headline must remain legible and the four ability images visibly distinct from generic lettered gamepad buttons.
- Check no clipping of app controls, overlap from editorial copy, invented live feedback, horizontal stretch, or skin/package changes.
- Preserve native raw captures, editable composition sources, exported dimensions/hashes, and a three-up contact sheet. Independent marketing visual review and QA should inspect the actual exports; existing skin review remains immutable evidence for the reused package.
- Draft review only. No App Store upload, publication, or human approval is authorized by this document.
